# Safety notes

## What this is

A hackathon demonstration of an **assistive** workflow that moves information between a patient and a clinician.

## What this is not

- Not a standalone medical diagnosis.
- Not a certified medical interpreter.
- Not a regulated medical device.
- Not a substitute for clinical judgement or emergency care.

## Demo-specific limits

- Sample clinical content is synthetic.
- Translations are curated examples, not clinically validated.
- The WAV files are placeholders, not natural speech.
- Mock STT reads a sidecar transcript instead of recognizing audio.

## Required before real use

1. Informed consent and language preference capture.
2. Clinician review of every structured packet and patient-facing reply.
3. Red-flag escalation pathway and emergency instructions.
4. Access control, encryption, retention limits, and audit logging.
5. Validation of translation quality with qualified reviewers.
6. Local regulatory / clinical governance review.
